# Understanding

Review and independent semantic classification share typed Self formation criteria. `CurrentStance` requires a narrow, reproducible conditional judgement with common reasons; `CurrentInterest` requires sustained attention after its initiating cue, supported by at least two independent later behavioral observations including a cue-independent return/connection or costly information seeking. Relative or forced choices alone remain preference evidence, without a new writable Preference kind. Supporting originals must intersect in meaning rather than combine unrelated reasons. Source turns carry an opaque conversation group for independence assessment; grouping is context for semantic review, not proof of independence.

Write Contract v0.2.3 states boundary calibration explicitly rather than adding ontology or pipeline layers: generalize minimally, keep the conditions the shared reason actually requires, drop scene-only detail such as one deadline value or place unless that detail is part of the reason, never merge different reasons by OR or lift them into one covering principle, require `AttachEvidence` to carry the target's whole substantive meaning without silently narrowing or broadening its boundary, treat unnecessary NoChange as a failure as well, and never attribute an observation the raw sources do not contain. These remain model-facing guidance; host provenance, schema, retrieval, authorization and lifecycle gates are unchanged. Calibration quality can only be established by real generated-scenario experiments, not by string assertions in tests.

The evidence compatibility invariant applies to `Create`, `AttachEvidence` and `Revise`: cited support must be compatible with the resulting entry's specific reason, required conditions and behavioral claim, and collectively support its whole meaning. Attach also requires direct support for an existing reason, condition or behavioral claim; similar outcomes alone do not qualify. Revision rechecks the revised meaning; preserved historical provenance does not automatically support a changed claim. Partial support calls for supported specialization, a separately bounded entry or no change, without an unsupported cross-domain union or an empty covering principle. This is shared review/classification guidance, with no change to host source retention or write authority.

Typed review candidates also pass an evidence validation gate before persistence, including saving Pending candidates in Proposals mode. The existing independent classification call returns a schema2 `support` verdict of `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, `UNSUPPORTED` or `CONTRADICTED`, separately from routing. Only `SUPPORTED` permits saving; missing, malformed, edited or differently bound assessments fail closed. Validation judges the exact proposed operation against sources and target meaning and cannot return replacement content. A justified withdrawal is supported as an operation, rather than automatically classified as contradicted because its target is false. Support does not grant approval, promotion, dialogue projection or a less restrictive effect. Review schema3/schema4, raw sources, provenance, entry storage and legacy host-written proposals retain their existing contracts.

Manual typed candidates may remain RAM-only previews when saving is disabled. Approval cannot save an unvalidated preview, and correcting a preview invalidates its prior assessment. Source/policy/target freshness checks and explicit approval remain independent requirements. Rejected candidates write only existing metadata receipts/coverage and safe diagnostic codes, without adding entry/revision payloads or mutating an old target. Existing saved history is not retroactively revalidated or cleaned up by this gate. Model verdict accuracy requires separate provider acceptance; deterministic gate tests do not establish semantic accuracy.

Initial user/world exposure or a persona seed does not permanently disqualify later experience-maintained attention. Persona instantiation remains no-op; elaboration needs explanatory or predictive surplus beyond the prior. These model-facing criteria do not replace host provenance, permissions, status or scope checks, and do not by themselves prove model judgement quality. Enacted and inferred proposals still begin as Hypothesis; no automatic Established or new desktop recurrence grant is introduced.

Write Contract v0.2.1 keeps the separate, non-projectable `RecurrenceClue` hint with a 14-day TTL and a per-scope capacity of 64. The detector proposes source aliases and bounded behaviour summaries; an independent entailment pass checks those summaries against current originals and external authorship. The host binds exact Assistant text, hashes and complete coverage. A separate, idempotent Stage A transaction rechecks source, scope, policy and input generations before saving validated clues. Later retrieval, review or classification failures do not roll back that transaction. It neither advances Self coverage/revision nor grants Self authority. Source changes, expiry, policy changes and conversation forgetting invalidate hints; restored backups discard them. Clues cannot create Self/facts/persona, enter ordinary/Native/query context, support Established, or replace original evidence; they remain excluded from the Understanding entry export.

`UnderstandingReviewService` can run separately budgeted second-order discovery before review/classification/write. Broad semantic recall selects up to eight hints, then causal reranking selects at most three comparable hints. It distinguishes time pressure, safety goals, evidence sufficiency and external authorship rather than equating surface actions. The coordinator rereads their exact originals through Kernel ports within the existing eight-original/6,000-scalar bound; excess material is logged and skipped. Only raw originals enter joint review. The isolated reliability host opts into schema4 source-alias expressions and compact classifier payloads with the same semantic classification rules. Legacy schema3 parsing and the default desktop review wire remain supported. `IRecurrenceIsolationReader` is a trusted host attestation of first-order context isolation, including absence of user reminders and same-task continuation; without it, independent historical pairing fails closed. The desktop source port still does not supply this attestation. Discovery consumes existing granted review dispatches and never enables processing or expands permissions. No Self threshold or promotion rule changes.

Write Contract v0.1 adds explicit `Origin` (`Expressed`, `Enacted`, `SecondOrderInferred`, `ExternallySuggested`) and `Status` independently of approval/effect/lifecycle. Automatic schema3 writes permit Self CurrentStance/CurrentInterest/Uncertainty/InteractionTendency/SelfObservation and Relationship InteractionPattern only. They cannot create UserFact, WorldFact, stable identity, permanent preferences, user psychology or Established status. Legacy host proposals/JSON retain their existing strict routing without being relabeled autonomous.

An Expressed Tentative candidate requires an exact quote from a complete host-read Assistant original. Enacted/inferred candidates start as Hypothesis. A promotion needs new complete Assistant originals later than the hypothesis in a different conversation; Enacted promotion needs two new independent conversations. These structural checks prove provenance, not semantic entailment or absence of influence from character instructions. Independent semantic classification still checks authorship, meaning, scope, counterevidence and user-suggested views. Repeated maintainer conclusions and source replays cannot promote.

The registered host supplies the active role's core persona and selected appearance overlay as separate externally authored context for both review and classification. It cannot be cited as an original or independent evidence. This helps distinguish an existing role rule from a newly expressed Self; exact quotations alone cannot establish autonomy. An explicitly unsupported semantic verdict produces no-op rather than another pending Self. Pending withdrawal proposals retain their intent and cannot be automatically maintained into ordinary stances.

Create/AttachEvidence/Revise/Retract reuse the existing transactional owner. Attach preserves meaning; revisions and retractions retain original sources/history and exact target dependencies. Evidence-supported changes that are not eligible for bounded activation remain separate pending replacements. A proposed withdrawal needs exact approval unless the bounded semantic gate admits it; approval withdraws the predecessor while retaining provenance. Maintenance includes saved pending hypotheses even when conversation use is off. Memory maintenance intents are advisory data only; Understanding never writes Memory or original evidence.

Hypothesis and ExternallySuggested entries never enter ordinary/Native/query context, even after user approval. Current understanding is scoped, active, unexpired and bounded by Self 3 / Relationship 2 / User 3 plus the existing whole-entry character budget. Approval does not establish truth or personality. Created time is `ObservedAt`; review time is `LastReviewedAt`, and revision links reference the retained owner revision history. Lifecycle dormancy remains separate from epistemic strength.

Owns derived interpretations, candidate revisions, exact-version local review, lifecycle, idempotent receipts and metadata-only attempt/coverage state. Original conversation messages belong to Dialogue and confirmed user facts belong to Memory. User-fact proposals return `UNDERSTANDING_MEMORY_OWNER_REQUIRED`; stable identity and permission changes return `UNDERSTANDING_DEFINITION_FLOW_REQUIRED`.

`Core/Contracts` publishes the reader and host-side review DTOs. `UnderstandingService` uses the shared runtime database and module migrations, validating source snapshots through Kernel's owner port. Receipt lookup precedes fresh validation. A connection data-version check and immediate write transaction reject intervening source commits without reading Dialogue private tables. A batch validates all dependencies before persisting anything.

The default mode is OFF with source access, external processing, saving and conversation use all ungranted. OFF previews are available for ten minutes in memory, pruned on subsequent owner access or session close; explicit exact-version approval can save a preview. Policy changes invalidate previews and candidate revisions. Unreviewed bounded entries stop on mode/grant changes and do not reactivate automatically; those changes preserve retracted/resolved lifecycle states. Self entries expire after seven days; authorized exact historical reads preserve their original time and dormant state. Approval preserves evidence strength. Routing requires review unless separate, bound semantic classification and structural checks both permit the limited BOUNDED path. Model classification is evidence for this routing, not human acceptance or general semantic correctness.

`UnderstandingReviewScheduler` and `UnderstandingReviewService` are registered by Desktop. `DailyReviewBudgetAccess` requires an explicit scope/recipient grant in addition to owner permissions. `DailyReviewLedger` stores hashed grants and precharges in `understanding-daily-budget.db`, separate from conversation export, forgetting and restored backups. The adjustable shared daily limit defaults to 12 dispatches across scopes and routes; review, classification and retries each consume a dispatch at the final HTTP handoff. Failed, cancelled and unknown attempts never refund quota. UTC rollover restores allowance; a clock rollback cannot mint allowance. Unsupported or corrupt stores fail closed. This bounds application-to-provider dispatches, not retries internal to a proxy. The original experimental `ReviewCallLedger` retains its immutable task/batch/trial approvals unchanged.

Events only mark activity. Dialogue's bounded metadata scan and Understanding's transactional cursor/queue recover missed notifications without storing original bodies in work records. Queue pressure retains the scan frontier. Long originals advance by actual Unicode-scalar coverage; mixed item outcomes stay in receipts. Recovery checks committed receipts before renewed input, permissions or retries. OFF starts no automatic work, and new automatic grants exclude earlier ranges through `auto_since`. A manual preview's display acknowledgement is explicit; a returned result alone does not mark its attempt as displayed. Process interruption never automatically replays an OFF preview.

The scheduler uses monotonic idle/age measurements: 120 seconds idle, 15 minutes oldest work, six completed turns or an approximate 6k-token threshold. A scope waits at least two minutes between attempts and five minutes after a failed result; retry counts remain bounded by the existing queue. The desktop background task bound remains one minute. A trusted host can explicitly supply a positive task deadline capped at two minutes; the isolated six-stage reliability experiment uses that allowance. This does not alter call ceilings, dispatch grants, foreground/provider timeouts or cancellation. Dialogue foreground leases preempt background work; maintenance cancels and drains it. Model calls run outside UI/commit transactions. A short owner transaction rechecks source, policy, input and target generations through synchronous HTTP handoff; the network await occurs after that transaction releases.

Asynchronous scheduler disposal cancels and drains both periodic work and explicit pulses, including a pulse made before periodic startup. Stopped instances cannot dispatch again. Cancellation keeps unfinished source ranges and the separate dispatch charge available for safe recovery.

`IReviewModel` and `UnderstandingReviewModel` implement the candidate protocol over Kernel's provider, request meter and stream aggregator. Schema 3 binds original aliases, exact selected target revisions, authorship, status, operation and a request fingerprint; source coverage retains the host-supplied slice. No tools are offered. The adapter enforces the trusted host's `ReviewRequestLimits` and valid terminal usage; absent an explicit override, the existing 16,000 input / 2,048 output limits apply. Foreground dispatch, review and semantic classification share that bound capacity. Partial, malformed or stale results never become candidates. It identifies the request as the `understanding_review` auxiliary purpose so the Dialogue wire owner can apply its endpoint-specific JSON policy. The host still owns source reads, current permissions, atomic dispatch handoff, durable accounting, scheduling and the subsequent owner commit. Constructing this adapter or supplying capacity does not enable a worker or grant external processing. The existing v4 ledger retains its immutable call ceilings; an explicitly authorized host may supply a different `IReviewCallBudget` without changing those historical approvals.

The registered daily host supplies route-resolved capacity to both review and validation. It reserves the connection's configured output (clamped to the model maximum) plus normal context headroom, without an additional fixed 16k input ceiling or hidden 2048 output clamp. The request meter remains conservative; requests exceeding the resolved model input allowance still fail before dispatch. The daily ledger accounts granted calls rather than applying another token estimate ceiling, and retains exact scope/route grants, daily count limits, precharging, revocation and no refund of uncertain calls. Original-window limits remain eight sources/6000 scalars. Direct legacy adapters and historical immutable experiment ledgers retain their separately specified limits; this does not rewrite old approvals or alter saved model settings.

The review template exposes the same owner restrictions as commit validation: the schema3 path permits only the limited Self/Relationship kinds and requires complete originals for Expressed attribution; UserFact belongs to Memory, and identity/permission use their dedicated flows. Unsupported material must be omitted rather than renamed to pass a boundary. Self uses the canonical subject `assistant`; the legacy World path retains subject `world` with an observed object's name in content. These instructions do not replace the owner checks or independent semantic classification.

`ReviewBudgetedChatProvider` publishes its task input ceiling through Kernel's `IChatInputLimit`, so ordinary/Native composition sees the same resolved allowance as the final handoff (or the explicit legacy allowance for older callers). Model capacity, configured output reservation, original timeout and tool/Persona contracts still apply. Optional records stay whole or are omitted; mandatory over-cap envelopes fail closed. A Self/World label cannot automatically replace an existing User/Relationship target.

Source revisions and read coverage are separate. The Dialogue authority validates a monotonic source revision in addition to the original fingerprint, preventing a completed/failed/completed transition from reviving an old reference. Host tickets bind source-version/scalar-range coverage independently of model proposals. Validation never upgrades unknown or partial coverage to a full read. Legacy host proposals with unattested coverage stay pending even when a test routing policy otherwise allows the content; schema3 Expressed attribution requires complete host-attested coverage.

Automatic attempt reservations require candidate-saving permission and at least one attested source revision after the host's `auto_since` boundary. The boundary is captured on OFF-to-PROPOSALS/BOUNDED changes and when a non-OFF mode receives or regains read permission. Other grant changes retain the existing boundary. Earlier authorized sources can accompany the new range as background; an old-only range or missing save grant reserves no attempt. Explicit manual ranges retain their independent read/model authorization and need not enable automatic accumulation or candidate saving.

`UnderstandingPlugin` provides ordinary prompt data, Native context and `understanding.query` over the same effective-state reader. Pending/rejected entries are excluded. Unauthorized, disabled, changed-source and unavailable outcomes are distinguished. Context entries include conditions, lifecycle, source and correction together and are retained or omitted whole. No write tool is registered.

`Desktop` owns one content-first Settings entry with Saved and To review views; Search exposes text/topic filters and the Archived view. Empty scopes show a single empty state. Saved is distinct from the current reply projection. Correct opens an in-memory draft and changes only the correction; pending entries still require exact-version approval. Details groups readable sources, coverage limitations, earlier wording and lifecycle actions without exposing internal source IDs or fingerprints. Forget confirms deletion of the entry and revision history while preserving original messages. Drafts, filters and disclosures survive page navigation and refresh within the WebView lifetime; changed scope/version bindings require explicit review before saving. Settings starts collapsed and contains automatic review, reply use and the independent activity controls. Failed or blocked reviews show a short status with diagnostics on demand; refresh queries status without dispatching review.

The host supplies readable role/project names, non-secret model recipient metadata and the current reply projection. Page context tokens bind mutations to the active scope, dialogue generation and policy generation, in addition to owner revisions. Valid mutations suspend/drain dialogue and recheck scope and cancellation before committing. The automatic setup command additionally binds the displayed model route and explicitly chosen daily limit. Reading, external processing, saving and conversation use remain independent choices. Proposals saves pending candidates; Bounded also permits independently classified low-impact effects. Pause and revocation preserve content; reply-use revocation immediately removes it from subsequent projection. Opening the page grants nothing and sends no model request. The page displays budget, queued sources and the latest processing result in the current process; restarting clears that transient display, but not dispatch accounting or queue receipts.

The daily host resolves actual configured model capacity before review and classification, reserving output and safety headroom. Each input is additionally capped at 16,000 tokens, output at 2,048 and the configured/provider output limit. A route change during preparation or dispatch is rejected; a new recipient needs a new explicit grant. These task limits do not change the foreground conversation window.

The same page hosts separate **Activity & background** settings through optional `ICompanionAwarenessControls`. Its activity summaries, application overview, selected-window permission and background request allowance belong to the App host, not `UnderstandingService`, its policy or its historical review ledger. Activity context defaults on for Focus/reminders and the shared local Todo book; the clock is independent. Application overview and background thought default off. Saving explicitly approves these choices for the current model; a model change requires renewed approval. The background allowance defaults to six calls per rolling 24 hours, with a host-supported range of 1–24 and 4,096 input / 384 output tokens per request, additional to chat and memory extraction. The simplified page displays and preserves the host's saved allowance when changing sources; a missing or invalid allowance blocks saving instead of inventing a default. It produces temporary context without a new user message, unsolicited expression or saved-understanding writes.

The four closed `understanding.awareness.configure/windows/allowWindow/clearWindows` commands use the controls owner's independent context token/revision and suspend/drain boundary. Window titles are enumerated only after an explicit click; a selected native source token permits repeated reads of that same window for the current app session, ending on scope/model/title changes, closure or revocation. Window titles and other source text are rendered as text, never markup. Opening or refreshing the page does not enumerate sources, grant access or enable background thought. A page without the optional host keeps its saved-understanding review behavior. Source sampling and ordinary/Native projection are described by [Desktop Context](../FgoPet.Plugin.DesktopContext/README.md); bounded model activity belongs to [Kernel](../../src/FgoPet.Kernel/README.md).

Privacy operations enter through Kernel's `IDerivedConversationData`: explicit export, forgetting a conversation/all derived data, and sanitizing an already-validated staged backup. Forgetting removes payloads and revision history, retaining only hashed revocations to prevent restoration from old backups. Restores merge current revocations, preserve retracted/resolved states from either the backup or current entries, reset all grants to OFF and mark other surviving entries dormant. Retracted entries cannot be approved, restored, suspended or resolved; correction/deletion retain their existing rules. No current database is normalized in place. The host owns maintenance and rollback.

The Desktop registration writes local operational metadata to `understanding-diagnostics.jsonl` beside `runtime.db` (the application state directory). Records contain time, a per-process-registration session identifier, hashed correlations, stages and safe reason codes; they exclude source text, prompts, model responses, credentials and exception messages. Worker startup/shutdown, source scanning, queue inspection, eligibility, model/budget checks, foreground admission, batch claims and completion/failure are recorded. Reasons distinguish disabled grants, empty/full queues, idle/age/size/turn thresholds, cooldowns, recipient authorization, daily limits, foreground activity and unavailable work. Start markers and stage-specific failures distinguish a stalled operation from a skipped one. Identical correlation/stage/reason events are sampled once per minute; changed reasons and failures are written immediately, and failures reset sampling so recovery is visible. The current file rotates at 2 MiB, keeping one `.previous` file. Logging failures never grant permission, consume model budget or change scheduling. These files are local troubleshooting records, not release assets or a source of authorization.

## Short-lived topic material

`TopicMaterialStore` owns a bounded external-snippet cache, separate from saved Understanding entries. Materials bind an exact effective `CurrentInterest` or `OpenQuestion` revision and role/project, retain source title/URL and fetch/expiry times, and expire after two days for the research worker (store maximum seven days). The default capacity is 64 per scope. A duplicate topic/revision and URL or summary does not renew its lifetime. Forgetting clears this cache conservatively for the role; backup normalization clears staged materials and merges current research accounting so restoring cannot refund attempts. Materials are excluded from derived-data export.

`MaterialResearchActivity` chooses at most one eligible topic, requests one query, invokes only the registered `web.search` provider through a narrow adapter, then requests at most one source-index-bound summary. It never calls a general tool registry, reads pages, sends unsolicited messages, or writes World entries. Each request is limited to 4,096 input tokens; query output is capped at 384 tokens and summary output at 768, further reduced by configured/provider limits. Summary headroom includes any provider reasoning. Truncated responses remain rejected with a safe protocol-specific diagnostic code. A research task has a 90-second deadline and no retry. The separate persistent allowance permits at most six research tasks per rolling 24 hours, at least five minutes apart and six hours apart for a topic. Each task can use two model calls and one search; failed or cancelled dispatched attempts remain charged. Foreground conversation cancels or defers this work through the existing dialogue lifetime.

Research defaults off. The App requires a separate `topic-material-research.json` grant in its storage directory, binding `Enabled`, exact `RoleId`/nullable `ProjectId`, `ApprovedRoute` (the complete current `ModelRouteKey`), `SearchProvider` (`duckduckgo`) and `DailyLimit` (1–6). It also requires current Understanding read/external-processing/save/dialogue-use grants and the existing web-search grant. The bundled keyless search helper is the only research source, so research never reads a separate credential. This experimental configuration has no settings-page toggle; opening Understanding or enabling ordinary web search does not enable it. Changing role, project, recipient, provider, policy or grant invalidates captured work. Do not distribute a machine's grant.

`TopicMaterialPlugin` supplies zero or one whole optional data block to ordinary and Native conversation. Current topic revisions, source grants and expiry are rechecked. Kernel reuses the existing conversation intent to suppress material for closure or task-only turns; Native tool steps omit the exact material block. The model may leave available material unused. `LastUsedAt` is only a weak completed-turn candidate-selection signal: a later budget or conversation gate may omit the block, and it never proves semantic use. Recently selected material is deprioritized, never permanently disabled. No new turn classifier or `TurnUnderstanding` production dependency is introduced.
